Material Trade-Off Dossier

Mechanical Flexibility expand_more

Inorganic (Wafer)

Rigid, brittle crystalline structure. Fracture strain is typically less than 1%. Requires flat, rigid encapsulation (glass).

Organic (Polymer)

Extremely flexible, rollable polymer films. Strain can exceed 15%, enabling bendable displays, smart textiles, and conformal aerospace arrays.

Exciton Binding & Dielectric Screening expand_more

Inorganic (High ε)

Strong dielectric screening (ε ≈ 12). Exciton binding energy is extremely weak (4-15 meV), allowing spontaneous thermal dissociation at room temperature.

Organic (Low ε)

Weak screening (ε ≈ 3-4). Exciton binding energy is strong (300-800 meV). Requires a heterojunction interface (Donor-Acceptor) with sufficient energy offset to overcome the Onsager-Braun barrier.

Fabrication Footprint expand_more

Inorganic

High-temperature processing (900-1400°C), requiring cleanrooms, vacuum lithography, and extensive energy budgets.

Organic

Solution printing, slot-die coating, and thermal evaporation at low temperatures (< 150°C). Drastically reduced thermal budget enabling continuous roll-to-roll manufacturing.
